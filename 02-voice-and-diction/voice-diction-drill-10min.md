# Voice & Diction Drill Script: 10-Minute Audio Guide

Overnight workflow step 6 of 11. Target length: 10 minutes. Read aloud at a relaxed pace; pause where marked.

## Min 00:00 - 02:00: Posture & Diaphragmatic Breath Release (복식호흡 & 이완)
- Stand or sit tall: feet hip-width, knees soft, crown of head lifted, jaw and shoulders loose.
- Place one hand on the lower ribs, one on the belly. Inhale through the nose for 4 counts, feeling the ribs expand sideways and back. Exhale on a soft "sss" for 6 counts.
- Repeat 5 cycles. On the last exhale, let the jaw drop and hum gently for 2 counts.
- Shake out the hands and arms for 10 seconds.

## Min 02:00 - 04:00: Articulatory Warm-up (조음기관 스트레칭 & 혀/입술 릴리즈)
- Lips: "brr" lip trill for 10 seconds, then big open-close lip stretches ("ooo-eee") x 8.
- Jaw: massage the masseter, then slow jaw circles x 5 each direction.
- Tongue: stick out and stretch forward 5 times, then up to the nose and down to the chin, 3 times each. Tongue-tip circles around the lips x 5 each direction.
- Tongue trill ("rrr") for 10 seconds.
- Soft palate: yawn-sigh, 2 times.

## Min 04:00 - 07:00: Resonant Vocalization & Pitch Ladders (공명 발성 & 모음 트레이닝: 아/에/이/오/우)
- Hum on "mmm" for 5 seconds, feeling buzz in the lips and nose. Slide into "ng" and back.
- Pitch ladder, 5-note scale (do-re-mi-fa-sol), on each vowel in order: 아 (ah), 에 (eh), 이 (ee), 오 (oh), 우 (oo). Sing each vowel on one breath, sustained 4 counts per note.
- Ascend one half step after each full vowel set, 2 sets total. Keep the sound forward, not pushed from the throat.
- Vowel focus: say each vowel 3 times in a sustained, resonant tone. Check that the jaw stays relaxed and the tongue stays low for 아 and high-forward for 이.
- Monologue-level projection: say "아-에-이-오-우" at speaking volume with full breath, 3 times.

## Min 07:00 - 09:00: Diction Drills (자음 정밀 발음 & 간장공장공장장 류 훈련 + 영어 plosives/fricatives)

### Korean consonant precision (자음 정밀 발음)
- Read slowly, then at speed, focusing on crisp consonants:
  - 간장공장 공장장은 강 공장장이고, 된장공장 공장장은 전 공장장이다.
  - 경찰청 철창살은 쇠창살이고, 검찰청 쇠창살은 철창살이다.
  - 내일 가는 옆집 앞 야경 구경 가자.
- Tense and aspirated sets: 가다 / 카다 / 까다, 다라 / 타라 / 따라. Make the difference clear between the three sounds.

### English plosives (p, b, t, d, k, g)
- "Pete picked a peck of pickled peppers." Repeat 3 times, then at speed.
- "Big black bugs bite better." Repeat 3 times.
- Contrast drill: "pat / bat, tack / dack, cap / gap". Focus on the burst of air for p, t, k. Keep b, d, g voiced with less burst.

### English fricatives (f, v, th, s, z, sh)
- "Fresh fish, fried fast, forever." Repeat 3 times.
- "Six slippery snails slid slowly seaward." Repeat 3 times.
- "Thin things, this other thing." Contrast th-voiceless and th-voiced, 3 times each.
- Hold each fricative ("fffff", "sssss", "vvvvv") for 5 seconds, keeping airflow steady.

## Min 09:00 - 10:00: Cold Monologue Beat Connection (감정 연결 한 줄 호흡 훈련)
- Choose one line from the Script Backlog. Read it cold, once, with no preparation.
- Breathe in on the thought, not on the word. Speak the full line on one exhale.
- Repeat the line 3 times, each time shifting the emotional intention (for example: asking, pleading, realizing). Let the breath change with each intention.
- Final pass: say the line in your natural performance voice, then pause for 3 seconds of silence before moving on.
- Close: one slow breath in for 4 counts, out for 6 counts.

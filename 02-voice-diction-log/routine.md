# Daily 10-Minute Vocal and Diction Routine

Run this as minutes 2 to 4 of the daily session. Use a timer. Do not exceed 2 minutes for the warmup portion. Stand up, feet hip-width, shoulders loose.

## 1. Diaphragmatic Breathing (about 30 seconds)

- Place one hand on the lower ribs and one on the upper chest.
- Inhale through the nose for a count of 4. Only the lower hand should move.
- Exhale on a steady "sss" for a count of 6.
- Repeat 4 times.

## 2. Phonation (about 30 seconds)

- Lip trill (motorboat) on a comfortable pitch, for 10 seconds. Keep the lips loose.
- Hum "mmm" on a single pitch, then slide up and down a fifth. Keep the sound forward in the mask.
- Stop if the throat tightens. Resume with breath.

## 3. Articulation Drill (about 60 seconds)

Say each line once slowly, once at speed, then once at performance pace. Focus on the consonant at the end of each word.

- "Red leather, yellow leather."
- "Unique New York, unique New York."
- "The sixth sick sheik's sixth sheep's sick."
- "Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers."

Track problem sounds in the log (for example: final t, the r in "leather," the s clusters).

## 4. Resonance and Pitch Check (about 20 seconds)

- Say "Hello, is anybody there?" with the pitch falling on "there."
- Repeat, placing the voice slightly forward, as if speaking to the back of the room.
- Note whether the sound feels forward or sits in the throat.

## Rules

- Never push volume above conversational level during warmup.
- Stop any exercise that causes pain. Note it in the log and move on.
- Warm up before the monologue, not after. The monologue is the test.
- If the warmup takes longer than 2 minutes, shorten the drills. Do not skip breathing.

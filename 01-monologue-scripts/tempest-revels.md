# The Tempest: Our revels now are ended

- Character: Prospero, the rightful Duke of Milan
- Situation: On his island, interrupting the masque he conjured for Ferdinand and Miranda because of Caliban's plot.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *The Tempest*, Act IV, Scene I.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1540](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1540). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

## What the speech does

Prospero stops a show he made for the young lovers and tells them that the world, like the show, will fade. A sudden shift from performance to private weariness.

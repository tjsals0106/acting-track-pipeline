# Twelfth Night: I left no ring with her

- Character: Viola, disguised as the page Cesario
- Situation: Alone, after Malvolio has thrown back a ring that Olivia secretly sent, and Viola realizes Olivia loves her in disguise.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *Twelfth Night*, Act II, Scene II.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1526](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1526). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.
- Rights: link only. The play (Shakespeare, d. 1616) is out of copyright, but this archive does not reproduce the text, because the rights status of the modernized digital edition outside the US was not verified. Description below was written for this archive.

## What the speech does

Viola, disguised as a man, realizes Olivia has fallen for her and works through the problem aloud. Light and quick, with real feeling underneath the comedy.

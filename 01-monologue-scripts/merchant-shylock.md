# The Merchant of Venice: Hath not a Jew eyes

- Character: Shylock, a moneylender in Venice
- Situation: Speaking to Salarino and Solanio, who mock him after his daughter has run away with his money.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *The Merchant of Venice*, Act III, Scene I.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1515](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1515). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

## What the speech does

Shylock answers the men who mock him by listing what he shares with them as a human being, then turns that sameness into a case for revenge. The speech moves from argument to menace.

# As You Like It: All the world's a stage

- Character: Jaques, a melancholy lord
- Situation: In the forest of Arden, speaking to the exiled Duke after Orlando has burst in asking for food.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *As You Like It*, Act II, Scene VII.

## What the speech does

Jaques lays out the stages of a human life as a set of roles, from infant to old age. A witty, reflective speech with a bleak end; watch for the changing tone of each age.

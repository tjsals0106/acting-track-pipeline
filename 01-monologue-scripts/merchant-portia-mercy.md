# The Merchant of Venice: The quality of mercy

- Character: Portia, disguised as a lawyer
- Situation: In the Venetian court, pleading with Shylock to spare Antonio before the judgment.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *The Merchant of Venice*, Act IV, Scene I.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1515](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1515). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

## What the speech does

Disguised as a lawyer in court, Portia asks Shylock to show mercy and describes mercy as stronger than law. A calm, public plea that is also a tactic.

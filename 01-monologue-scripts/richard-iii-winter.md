# Richard III: Now is the winter of our discontent

- Character: Richard, Duke of Gloucester
- Situation: Alone in a London street, after the wars have ended and his brother Edward is king.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *Richard III*, Act I, Scene I.

## What the speech does

Richard speaks to the audience after the wars end, mocking peacetime and his own body, then announces that he will make himself a villain. Charm and menace shared directly with the house.

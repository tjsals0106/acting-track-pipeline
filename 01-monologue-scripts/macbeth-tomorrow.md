# Macbeth: Tomorrow, and tomorrow

- Character: Macbeth, Thane of Glamis and King of Scotland
- Situation: He has just been told that Lady Macbeth is dead while enemy forces close in on his castle.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *Macbeth*, Act V, Scene V.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1533](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1533). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.
- Rights: link only. The play (Shakespeare, d. 1616) is out of copyright, but this archive does not reproduce the text, because the rights status of the modernized digital edition outside the US was not verified. Description below was written for this archive.

## What the speech does

Macbeth hears of his wife's death and answers with a short, flat meditation on time and the emptiness of life. Play it as exhaustion, not rage: the speech is a retreat from feeling.

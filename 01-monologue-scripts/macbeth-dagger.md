# Macbeth: Is this a dagger

- Character: Macbeth, Thane of Glamis
- Situation: Alone at night in Inverness castle, on his way to murder the sleeping king.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *Macbeth*, Act II, Scene I.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1533](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1533). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

## What the speech does

Alone before the murder, Macbeth sees a floating dagger and argues with his own imagination while the night and the bell push him toward the act. A speech of rising fear that tips into resolve.

# Othello: It is the cause

- Character: Othello, a general of Venice
- Situation: In the bedchamber at night, standing over the sleeping Desdemona before he kills her.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *Othello*, Act V, Scene II.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1531](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1531). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

## What the speech does

Othello stands over his sleeping wife, tells himself the killing is a matter of justice, and cannot stop looking at her. Quiet, controlled, with cracks as tenderness breaks through.

# A Midsummer Night's Dream: How happy some o'er other some can be

- Character: Helena
- Situation: Alone in the Athenian wood at night, after Demetrius has run from her toward Hermia.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *A Midsummer Night's Dream*, Act I, Scene I.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1514](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1514). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

## What the speech does

Helena, alone, complains that love is blind and unfair, since Demetrius ignores her though she is as attractive as Hermia. Comic self-pity that turns into a plan.

# Hamlet: O, what a rogue and peasant slave am I

- Character: Hamlet, Prince of Denmark
- Situation: Alone after the travelling players have left, having watched an actor weep for Hecuba.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *Hamlet*, Act II, Scene II.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1524](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1524). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

- Status: Backlog

## What the speech does

After watching an actor weep over a story, Hamlet attacks himself for doing nothing, then decides to test the king with a play. A self-beating build with a clear turn into a plan.

# Henry V: What's he that wishes so?

- Character: King Henry V
- Situation: The morning before Agincourt, outnumbered, when Westmoreland wishes for more men.
- Source: William Shakespeare, *Henry V*, Act IV, Scene III.
- Where to read: [Project Gutenberg eBook #1521](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1521). Find the scene above and find the speech containing the title words.

## What the speech does

Before a battle where his army is outnumbered, the king refuses extra men and tells the camp that those who fight will be honored for the rest of their lives. A speech for a crowd; he is selling courage.

## Rights

These works are in copyright. Only links to copies legitimately published by the broadcaster or with the studio's authorization are provided; no text is reproduced on this site. Modern musical-theater libretti are rarely released this way; for those, the licensing houses (for example Music Theatre International and Concord Theatricals) sell perusal copies instead of publishing free scripts.
